# Brand

Current round: [**round 5**](round-5/README.md) — the symbol is settled (the divider, now with a pivot hole at the hinge) and the wordmark typeface is being chosen among sans-serifs with character, all optically centred on the V. Palette approved since round 1: paper `#F6F1EA`, ink `#2B2A28`, accent `#1F6F6B` (dark theme `#17161A`, `#EDE8E0`, `#5FB3AE`).

Earlier rounds, kept for reference:

- [`round-1/`](round-1/): converging lines (A), time rings (B), typographic V (C) and a wordmark in a humanist face. Declined 2026-09-23: none of the marks; the typeface should have symmetric letterforms.
- [`round-2/`](round-2/): loupe on a V stand (D), five observations forming a V (E), focus frame (F) and twelve wordmarks with uniform tracking. E was chosen as a direction; D and F declined; the wordmarks lacked real kerning and consistent sizing.
- [`round-3/`](round-3/): six parametric variations of E and five geometric wordmarks. Declined 2026-09-23: none of the variants; the V looked displaced (the word's halves have different widths); the geometric faces lacked personality.
- [`round-4/`](round-4/): fresh symbols (divider, tag, eye), the V optically centred, six characterful faces mostly serif. **The divider (S1) was chosen**; the centring was approved; the client asked for sans-serif faces instead.
