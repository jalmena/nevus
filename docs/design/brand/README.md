# Brand

The identity lives in [`identity/`](identity/README.md): the divider mark, the `neVus` wordmark in B612 Bold with the mark as its V, Epilogue for the interface, and the palette (paper `#F6F1EA`, ink `#2B2A28`, accent `#1F6F6B`; the brand block is always shown on the dark paper `#17161A` with ink `#EDE8E0` and accent `#5FB3AE`).

Settled on 2026-09-23 after five rounds of exploration with the Product Owner; the exploratory rounds were removed from the repository at the Product Owner's request.
