# Brand

**Settled identity: [`identity/`](identity/README.md)** (2026-09-23): the divider mark, the `neVus` wordmark in B612 Bold with the mark as its V, Epilogue for the interface, and the palette approved since round 1 (paper `#F6F1EA`, ink `#2B2A28`, accent `#1F6F6B`; dark `#17161A`, `#EDE8E0`, `#5FB3AE`).

Rounds, kept for reference:

- [`round-1/`](round-1/): converging lines (A), time rings (B), typographic V (C) and a wordmark in a humanist face. Declined: none of the marks; the typeface should have symmetric letterforms.
- [`round-2/`](round-2/): loupe on a V stand (D), five observations forming a V (E), focus frame (F) and twelve wordmarks with uniform tracking. E was chosen as a direction; D and F declined; the wordmarks lacked real kerning and consistent sizing.
- [`round-3/`](round-3/): six parametric variations of E and five geometric wordmarks. Declined: none of the variants; the V looked displaced (the word's halves have different widths); the geometric faces lacked personality.
- [`round-4/`](round-4/): fresh symbols (divider, tag, eye), the V optically centred, six characterful mostly-serif faces. **The divider was chosen**; the centring was approved; sans-serif faces requested.
- [`round-5/`](round-5/): the divider with a pivot hole; six sans-serif faces with character. **B612 chosen for the wordmark, Epilogue for the interface**; the hinge hole set to half the disc's diameter; the mark itself to stand in for the V.
